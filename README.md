# Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## Overview
An Excel dashboard that summarises 2025 sales performance across five Nigerian regions. It turns 150 raw orders into a one-page executive summary showing revenue, quantity sold, regional performance, product performance and monthly trends.

## Key Metrics
- **Total orders:** 150
- **Total revenue:** ₦210,041.26
- **Total quantity sold:** 776

## Dataset
The data covers Jan to Dec 2025 and has 12 fields: Order ID, Customer Name, Gender, Region, Product Category, Product Name, Quantity, Unit Price, Total Sales, Order Date, Payment Method and Sales Rep.

## Data Cleaning
- Standardised inconsistent text entries, such as "lagos" and "Lagos", and "cash", "Cash" and "CASH".
- Added a Months column from Order Date for the monthly trend analysis.

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for orders, revenue and quantity
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by region
- Sales by product category
- Top 5 performing products
- Interactive slicers for Gender, Payment Method and Order Date (by quarter)

## Key Insights
- **Lagos** is the top region at ₦65,379.41, and **Port Harcourt** is the lowest at ₦21,515.77.
- **Electronics** is the best-selling category, followed by Home Appliances, Groceries and Fashion.
- **Laptop** is the top product, followed by Fan, Headset, Shirt and Phone.
- **Cash** is the most common payment method, at about 59% of revenue.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Slicers, Charts, Data Cleaning)
